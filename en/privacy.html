Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

PulseLog is a blood pressure and blood sugar diary for iPhone, made by Indise LLC (“we”). The short version: we don’t collect any data. Your readings stay on your iPhone.

In short

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • We run no servers. PulseLog never sends your readings to us or anyone else.
  • The App Store privacy label for PulseLog is “Data Not Collected”.

What PulseLog is, and what it isn’t

PulseLog logs the readings you type in, for example from your own blood pressure monitor or glucose meter. It does not measure blood pressure or blood glucose; the iPhone camera and sensors are not used for that. PulseLog is not a medical device and nothing in it is medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your readings.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter (readings, notes, reminders and settings) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s private storage. That storage is shared with PulseLog’s own widgets (an App Group container) so they can show your latest reading. We can’t see any of it, and the app does not upload it to iCloud or any other cloud service.

Apple Health (optional)

When you set up PulseLog, the app asks for permission to read and write blood pressure and blood glucose. You decide exactly what to allow, and you can change it at any time in the Health app or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

  • Write: new readings are also saved to Apple Health, as a backup.
  • Read: if PulseLog’s storage is empty, for example after you reinstall it, the app can restore your readings from Apple Health.

Health data is used only to show and back up your readings inside PulseLog. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with anyone, and PulseLog never stores it in iCloud. Apple Health itself is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Exports and doctor reports

PulseLog can create PDF and CSV files of your readings. They are created on your iPhone and only leave it when you choose to share them, for example by AirDrop, email or a messaging app. After that, the file is handled by whoever or whatever you sent it to.

Weekly summary with Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, PulseLog Pro can write a plain-language weekly summary of your log. It is generated entirely on your iPhone by Apple’s on-device model. Your readings are not sent to us or to any server to create it. The summary describes your log; it is not a diagnosis or medical advice.

Reminders and alarms

Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone using Apple’s notifications and alarms. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Purchases

PulseLog Pro is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, card details or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple (StoreKit) whether your subscription is active.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That is controlled by you in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements; PulseLog adds nothing of its own.

Children

PulseLog is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Your data lives on your device, so you are in control: delete single readings in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Readings that were copied to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete, but you are always welcome to ask us questions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. We don’t have your contact details, so we can’t notify you personally.
