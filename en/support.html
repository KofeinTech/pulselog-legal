PulseLog Support

PulseLog is a simple diary for blood pressure and blood sugar. You type in the readings from your own monitor or meter, and PulseLog keeps them organized, shows charts and ranges, reminds you when it’s time to measure and creates a report for your doctor.

PulseLog logs readings you enter. It does not measure blood pressure or glucose, and it is not medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Email dimonzalis@gmail.com. It helps if you mention your iPhone model and the app version (shown in the app’s Settings).

Frequently asked questions

Is PulseLog free?

Yes. Unlimited logging, history, the 30-day chart, one reminder, the small widget and Apple Health backup and restore are free. PulseLog Pro adds PDF/CSV reports for your doctor, unlimited reminders and alarms, all-time trends, the medium, quick-log and Lock Screen widgets, the on-device weekly summary and custom ranges.

mg/dL or mmol/L?

PulseLog shows blood sugar in mg/dL by default. To switch to mmol/L, open Settings in the app and change the glucose unit. Your readings are converted on screen, nothing is lost, and you can switch back any time (1 mmol/L is about 18 mg/dL).

Where do the ranges come from?

Blood pressure categories follow the 2017 American Heart Association (AHA/ACC) guideline. Blood sugar ranges follow the American Diabetes Association (ADA). Your doctor may give you different targets; with Pro you can set your own ranges.

How do I back up my readings?

Your readings are stored only on your iPhone, so it’s worth keeping a backup:

  • Apple Health: allow Apple Health when PulseLog asks during setup, or turn on Health sync later in Settings. Every reading is also saved to Apple Health. If you reinstall PulseLog or move to a new iPhone, the app restores your readings from Health when its own storage is empty.
  • CSV file (Pro): export a CSV and keep it in Files or send it to yourself. You can import it again later from Settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, then open Settings in PulseLog and tap Restore Purchases. If Pro still doesn’t appear, check that the subscription is active in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, then email us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > PulseLog and tap Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my data?

  • One reading: open it in History and delete it.
  • Everything: delete the PulseLog app. That removes all of its data from your iPhone. We don’t have a copy, so this can’t be undone.
  • Apple Health: readings saved to Health stay there. To remove them, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, choose PulseLog and delete its data.

Reminders and alarms

The free version includes one reminder. Pro gives you unlimited reminders, plus alarms that ring even when your iPhone is on silent or in a Focus, so you don’t miss a reading. Allow notifications and alarms when PulseLog asks. If you said no earlier, turn them on in Settings > Apps > PulseLog.

Not getting a reminder? Check that it is switched on in the app, that notifications are allowed, and that a Focus isn’t silencing PulseLog.

I don’t see the weekly summary.

The weekly summary is part of Pro and needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones the option is hidden. The summary is written on your device and is not medical advice.

Does PulseLog collect my data?

No. There is no account, everything works offline and your readings stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
